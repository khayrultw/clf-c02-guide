# Suggested five-week student study plan

This is suggested pacing, not an AWS course requirement. Use short sessions with a mix of reading, retrieval from memory, and question review. If you cannot access Skill Builder activities, continue with the public sources and package lessons.

| Week | Study and explain | Practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read the [exam overview](00-exam-overview.md), [scope guide](05-in-scope-out-of-scope.md), and [cloud concepts lesson](01-domain-1-cloud-concepts.md). Explain each Well-Architected pillar with a different example. | Try the Official Practice Question Set if accessible, or use the local pilot as a diagnostic. Keep explanations separate until done. |
| 2 | Study [security and compliance](02-domain-2-security-compliance.md). Draw columns for AWS and customer duties on EC2, RDS, and Lambda. | Explain the closest alternative for each audit, monitoring, and threat-detection service. |
| 3 | Study [technology and services](03-domain-3-cloud-technology-services.md). Practice describing a service without using its name. | Work through scenarios from a fresh set. Record wrong answers and lucky guesses. |
| 4 | Study [billing and support](04-domain-4-billing-pricing-support.md) and the [comparison tables](06-service-comparison-tables.md). | Sort cases into estimate, analyze, alert, export, or seek help. Revisit missed Domain 2 and 3 topics. |
| 5 | Use the [readiness checklist](07-final-revision-checklist.md) and [exam traps](08-common-exam-traps.md). | Take the website's timed mock exam (65 questions, 90 minutes, no feedback until you submit) under exam conditions, then review every miss and lucky guess. Sets 11 and 12 contain mostly challenging items for a final check. Look at consistency and quality of explanations rather than treating a practice score as an AWS prediction. |

AWS lists getting familiar with the exam, refreshing knowledge, reviewing and practicing, and assessing readiness as preparation steps. Its [Skill Builder preparation page](https://skillbuilder.aws/category/exam-prep/cloud-practitioner-foundational-CLF-C02) and [Official Practice Question Set page](https://skillbuilder.aws/learn/E4W52ZKK6P/official-practice-question-set-aws-certified-cloud-practitioner-clfc02--english/RJSZKD3MG3) are optional supplements. Signed-in activity contents were not verified here.
